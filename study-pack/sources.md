## Errata you should open with the paper

The 2026 outline says “including errata” for Hurlimann, Meyers, Sahasrabuddhe, Shapland, Taylor, Venter, and Verrall. Check the CAS errata page / monograph pages before you memorize a formula that was later corrected.

## Suggested download order for a flight

If you can grab only four PDFs before the cabin door closes:

1. Friedland (A15–A17 is a large, self-contained chunk)
2. Mack 1994 + Mack 2000 (short, high hit-rate)
3. Clark (point estimate plus process variance in one paper)
4. Shapland or Taylor (pick the one you are weaker on)
