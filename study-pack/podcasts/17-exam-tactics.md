# Episode 17 — Cross-paper tactics

**Hosts:** Riley and Sam  
**Length:** about 18 minutes  
**Tasks:** A1–A17

---

**Riley:** Last episode. I want a decision tree I can run in the first ninety seconds of a case.

**Sam:** First: name the basis. Paid or incurred. Ground-up, limited, or excess. Loss or loss-and-ALAE. Accident year or treaty year. If you skip this, A4 and A16 are already wounded.

Second: A1. Volume, incrementals, latest diagonal, moon-cell, mix. If the latest diagonal is a stripe, you do not start with chain ladder.

Third: what is the question actually asking? Unpaid, ultimate, premium asset, percentile, range, risk margin, ceded unpaid. Those are different objects.

**Riley:** Point estimate, decent triangle, no CY stripe.

**Sam:** Chain ladder is legal. Still show BF or Cape Cod on the young years. If they disagree, Benktander with Z equals p, or Brosius if the year is thin. Clark if they want a curve and an SE.

**Riley:** Point estimate, young year, good premium, shaky reported.

**Sam:** BF or Cape Cod or Clark Cape Cod. Benktander sits near BF because p is small. Do not CL a twelve-month year.

**Riley:** Layer question.

**Sam:** Sahasrabuddhe conversion. Same size model for ILFs, trend, and LDFs. Siewert if it is WC deductible. Never apply the ground-up LDF to the excess layer and walk away.

**Riley:** Retro book.

**Sam:** dP equals CF times TM times dL, haircut for max and min. Same loss ultimate on both sides. If everyone is at max, premium asset on future development is zero.

**Riley:** They want a standard error or a percentile.

**Sam:** Mack if Venter passed and you only need an SE. Say process plus parameter. Do not add year SEs. If they want a percentile, name a distribution or go bootstrap / GLM / Bayes.

If Venter failed, do not quote Mack. Change the mean function.

Taylor: write the GLM, test a CY term, then quote prediction error.

Shapland: six steps, name the adjustments you actually need, diagnostics before the ninetieth.

Meyers: CSR if paid and speed changed. CAY if incurred and years moved together. Predictive distribution is posterior times process. Do not use Monograph 1.

**Riley:** They want a range.

**Sam:** Not CL to BF scribbled in the margin. Either percentiles from a distribution that includes the selection — Verrall, tau named — or a mix of models, Shapland-style, with weights you can defend. Sensitivity to tau or to the CY term is a better last paragraph than “I picked the midpoint.”

**Riley:** They want a risk margin.

**Sam:** Marshall framework. Independent plus systemic. The triangle does not contain legislative risk. Convert at a stated standard. If they want Meyers’ version, it is cost of capital on a Bayesian predictive distribution after dependence. A Mack seventy-fifth is not a risk margin.

**Riley:** Reinsurance case.

**Sam:** Cedent: push gross through the contract. Reinsurer: BF / exposure / Cape Cod, slower lag. Restate structure before you triangle. Split cat. Aggregate cover is an option. Reinstatement premium if the layer burns. Net versus a plausible retention.

**Riley:** Spreadsheet hygiene.

**Sam:** Pearson is not full Excel. Link the yellow cell. Keep one tab that is “selected pattern” so later case items do not drift. If a formula needs a tail or a last sigma, write the rule in a comment cell if you can, or in the constructed-response box.

**Riley:** Constructed-response last sixty seconds.

**Sam:** Did I answer the object they asked for? Is the basis consistent? If I mixed methods, is the weight between zero and one and named? If I reported a percentile, did I name the distribution? If I reported a margin, did I mention systemic risk?

**Riley:** Official practice.

**Sam:** After you land, download the July twenty twenty-six practice items from the Exam 7 page. Item 3 was revised July 14. Time the spreadsheet items first. Then open solutions and write one sentence: I lost points because blank. Replay the matching episode.

**Riley:** The cast, once, fast.

**Sam:** Brosius, Z from VHM and EVPV. Benktander, Z equals p. Hürlimann, same family, no proofs, reproducible collective LR. Clark, G of t, Cape Cod SE. Sahasrabuddhe, S-ratio. Siewert, WC excess develops longer. Teng, dP equals CF TM dL. Mack, three assumptions, do not add SEs. Venter, intercept and CY. Taylor, CL is a GLM, chapters one through six. Shapland, bootstrap plus adjustments. Meyers eight, CSR and CAY and CoC. Verrall, tau. Marshall, systemic. Friedland, push-through versus BF.

**Riley:** I am going to sleep. One sentence.

**Sam:** Name the basis, fix the triangle, pick the method that matches the failure, and do not quote a precise SE on a model you already know is wrong.

**Riley:** See you on the next flight. Work a triangle when the wheels are down.
